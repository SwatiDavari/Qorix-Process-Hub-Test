Policies
########

Organisation level policy statements: the "why" behind each discipline's detailed requirements.
Every Qorix project falls under them. A project may tailor how it satisfies a policy, never
whether it does.

Each policy is driven by business objectives (see :ref:`qx_objectives`), names the standard clauses it rests on
(all in the Needs catalogue), and drives the outcomes of the processes that carry it out. Source: ``governance/policies``
in the ``qorix-gnc`` repository.

The :ref:`qx_policy_register`, generated from ``needs/policies.yaml``, lists each policy with its standards,
work product, assembly and the outcomes it drives.
