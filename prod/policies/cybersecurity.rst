Cybersecurity Policy
####################

Statement
=========

Qorix maintains a Cybersecurity Management System (CSMS) per ISO/SAE 21434 clause 5. It treats
cybersecurity as a continuous organisational responsibility, not a one-time product gate. The
CSMS covers governance, culture, information sharing, tool management, information security
management, audit and continuous monitoring.

Driven by
=========

Business objectives (see :ref:`qx_objectives`): ``obj_qx_gc_security_continuous``, ``obj_qx_gc_skills_available``.

Scope
=====

Every project developing or maintaining a road vehicle system, component or software with
cybersecurity relevance.

Product-level threat analysis
=============================

This policy governs the organisation's CSMS. Product-level threat analysis and risk assessment
(TARA) is kept with the product, not in the hub.

Where it lives in the hub
=========================

* Standard: ISO/SAE 21434:2021 5.5.1 [WP-05-01] (``std_wp_isosae21434_org_management_551``).
* Work product: ``wp_qx_sec_policies``, maintained by the Security assembly (``compliance/security``).
* Detailed requirements: ``governance/cybersecurity/org_cybsec_requirements`` in ``qorix-gnc``.
  They are not yet in the hub.
