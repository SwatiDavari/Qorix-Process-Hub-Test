Safety Policy
#############

Statement
=========

Qorix treats functional safety as a non-negotiable organisational value, independent of any
single product's schedule or budget pressure. No product-level safety work (HARA, safety goals,
FSR, TSR, SSR) is valid unless the organisational prerequisites are in place first, per
ISO 26262 Part 2.

Driven by
=========

Business objectives (see :ref:`qx_objectives`): ``obj_qx_gc_safety_first``, ``obj_qx_gc_tailor_not_fork``, ``obj_qx_gc_skills_available``.

Scope
=====

Every project developing or maintaining a safety-related E/E system under Qorix, whatever the ASIL.

Ownership
=========

Functional safety organisational requirements are owned at organisation level. A project may
tailor how it satisfies them, never skip them. Tailoring goes through the project tier, with a
rationale for each narrowing.

Where it lives in the hub
=========================

* Standard: ISO 26262:2018 Part 2, 5.5.1 (``std_wp_iso26262_management_551``).
* Work product: ``wp_qx_saf_policies``, maintained by the Safety assembly (``compliance/safety``).
* Detailed requirements (safety culture, safety management system, competence, QMS alignment,
  confirmation measures, field monitoring): ``governance/functionalsafety/org_fusa_requirements``
  in ``qorix-gnc``. They are not yet in the hub.
