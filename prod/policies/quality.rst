Quality Policy
##############

Statement
=========

Qorix's quality management process (Automotive SPICE SUP.1 Quality assurance) applies the
Automotive SPICE process reference and assessment model across all projects, at a defined and
audited capability level. Tailoring is governed centrally, not left to individual project
discretion.

Driven by
=========

Business objectives (see :ref:`qx_objectives`): ``obj_qx_gc_process_capability``, ``obj_qx_gc_tailor_not_fork``, ``obj_qx_gc_skills_available``.

Scope
=====

Every project under Qorix's Automotive SPICE process umbrella.

Product-level evidence
======================

This policy governs the organisational process. Product-level review and metric evidence for
work products is kept with the product, not in the hub.

Where it lives in the hub
=========================

* Standards: Automotive SPICE 4.0 SUP.1 (``std_req_aspice_40_sup_1``) and ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288
  6.2.5 Quality management (``std_req_iso15288_625``). The hub's Quality Assurance assembly
  applies it to projects (15288 6.3.8).
* Work product: ``wp_qx_qa_qms_plan``, maintained by ``engineering/quality_assurance``.
* Detailed requirements (capability level policy, SUP.1 process, competence, appraisal programme,
  tailoring rules, safety and cybersecurity interface): ``governance/quality/org_quality_requirements``
  and ``governance/aspice/org_aspice_requirements`` in ``qorix-gnc``. They are not yet in the hub.
