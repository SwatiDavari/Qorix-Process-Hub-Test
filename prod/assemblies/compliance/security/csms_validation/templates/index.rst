   :status: draft

.. gd_req:: Threat-scenario classification
   :id: gd_req_qx_csval_threat_scenario
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_validation

   Every threat scenario in the applicable UNECE R155 Annex 5 listing shall be classified as not relevant, not in scope, vulnerable, or to be tested, and the classification shall be recorded in Validation Test Case Specification.

.. gd_req:: Penetration testing of vulnerable and to-be-tested threat scenarios
   :id: gd_req_qx_csval_penetration_testing_of
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_validation

   Threat scenarios classified as vulnerable or to be tested shall be exercised through penetration testing to confirm exploitability, and findings shall be recorded in Penetration Test Report (Internal / External). Where higher rigor, independence, or expertise is required for the CAL of the realized cybersecurity goal(s), penetration testing may be delegated to Cybersecurity Penetration Test Vendor.

.. gd_req:: Traceability of validation results
   :id: gd_req_qx_csval_traceability_of_val_results
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_validation

   Bidirectional traceability shall be established between the cybersecurity goals, the risk treatment validation strategy / specification, and the validation results, consistency between them shall be confirmed, and the summarized results shall be recorded in Cybersecurity Validation Report for communication to all affected parties.
