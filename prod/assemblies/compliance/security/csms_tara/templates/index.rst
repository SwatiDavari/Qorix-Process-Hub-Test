.. gd_req:: TARA update triggers
   :id: gd_req_qx_cstara_tara_update_triggers
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_tara

   A change impact analysis shall be started against the baselined TARA Record on any of: a change to item architecture, functionality, or operational environment; a new or changed cybersecurity requirement received from an OEM, customer, regulation, supplier, or internal stakeholder; a confirmed vulnerability not covered by an existing threat scenario or attack path. Cybersecurity requirements derived from this TARA's own cybersecurity goals are not a trigger. Where the change adds, changes, or removes a function, interface, or operational environment element, CS Item / Component Definition shall be updated and re-baselined before the TARA is re-analyzed (see tara_update_examples).

.. gd_req:: TARA change impact analysis and closure
   :id: gd_req_qx_cstara_tara_change_impact_analysis
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_tara

   Every change impact analysis shall be recorded in TARA Review Record (item 23 of Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment Review Checklist) with the trigger, its source, the affected TARA records, and the update decision with rationale, decided by Cybersecurity Product Owner and approved by Cybersecurity Manager. Where an update is required, re-analysis shall start at the first TARA section that the change touches and continue through the risk treatment decision, as defined in tara_update_rerun_scope. The analysis shall close only when these records are updated, every changed risk treatment decision, CAL, or goal / claim link is handed to Cybersecurity Goals and Claims with an Issue Tracking ID, and the updated TARA Record is re-reviewed, quality checked, and re-baselined. Where no update is required, the decision shall be recorded in a new version of TARA Review Record with only item 23 completed, referring to the unchanged TARA baseline.
