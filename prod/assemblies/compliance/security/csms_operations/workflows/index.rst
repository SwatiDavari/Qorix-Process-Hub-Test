   Incident data is gathered from field reports, customer notifications, or continuous monitoring, and assessed against the criteria and guidelines established in the incident management master plan. An event is classified as a cybersecurity incident when it involves actual or suspected exploitation of a vulnerability, unauthorized access, or unexpected behavior with a plausible cybersecurity cause, and is traceable to an asset or threat scenario considered in the item / component TARA. All analysis must be logged back to incident tracking system for further action. .. note:: 1. Where classification is uncertain, the event is provisionally logged as a candidate incident until triage confirms or rejects it. A valid incident is logged in the incident tracking system, categorized by severity and priority per Severity-based response timeline, and handed to Software Architect / Lead Engineer for response planning, subject to Cybersecurity Manager compliance approval. 2. Incidents logged by product / project team through customer interactions or inputs shall also be considered for analysis.
