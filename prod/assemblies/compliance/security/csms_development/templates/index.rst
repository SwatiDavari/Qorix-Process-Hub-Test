   When selecting a programming language or design modelling notation, a qualitative evaluation of its suitability for secure implementation shall be performed against the criteria described in ISO/SAE 21434 section 10.4.1, and the evaluation shall be recorded in Documentation of Modelling, Design, and Programming Language Guidelines.

   :id: gd_req_qx_csdev_closure_of_language_and
   :status: draft
   :version: 1

   Any gap identified in the selected language or modelling notation against the criteria of Evaluation of modelling, design, and programming languages shall be closed by appropriate design, modelling, or coding guidelines and by the development environment, and recorded in Documentation of Modelling, Design, and Programming Language Guidelines.
