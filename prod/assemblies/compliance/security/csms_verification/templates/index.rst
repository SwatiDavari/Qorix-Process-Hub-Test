   :status: draft
   :version: 1

   Integration and verification activities shall verify that the implementation and integration of components fulfil the defined cybersecurity specifications, and results shall be recorded in Verification Report for the Refined Specifications.

.. gd_req:: Verification specification criteria
   :id: gd_req_qx_csver_ver_spec_criteria
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_verification

   Integration and verification activities shall be specified in Integration and Verification Specification considering the defined cybersecurity specifications, the intended configuration (if applicable), sufficient capability to support the specified functionality, and conformance with the applicable modelling, design, and coding guidelines.

.. gd_req:: Test coverage evaluation
   :id: gd_req_qx_csver_test_coverage_evaluation
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_verification

   Where verification is performed by testing, test coverage shall be evaluated using defined test coverage metrics to determine the sufficiency of the test activities, and the evaluation shall be recorded in Verification Report for the Refined Specifications.

.. gd_req:: Minimization of unidentified weaknesses and vulnerabilities
   :id: gd_req_qx_csver_minimization_of_unidentified
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: csms_verification

   Testing, including fuzz testing scaled to the CAL of the realized cybersecurity goal(s), should be performed to confirm that unidentified weaknesses and vulnerabilities remaining in the component are minimized, and findings shall be recorded in Vulnerability Analysis Report.
