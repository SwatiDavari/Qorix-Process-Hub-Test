.. doc_getstrt:: Getting started on Safety
   :id: doc_getstrt_qx_saf_getting_started
   :status: valid
   :version: 1
   :tags: safety

   This describes how to get started with the parts of functional safety management that belong to the organisation rather than to a project — ISO 26262-2 clause 5 "Overall safety management".

   Four strands make up this area: (1) maintaining the Functional Safety Management System, (2) performing independent assessments, (3) managing safety anomalies, and (4) tailoring rules for project-level safety plans.
