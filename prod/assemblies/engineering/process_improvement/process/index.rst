Process Improvement and Tailoring

Turn audit findings, metrics and feedback into process improvements, and record how a project tailors the processes and why.

   This describes how the Qorix processes are improved and how a project tailors them.

   Improvement inputs come from audits, metrics, problems and project feedback. A tailoring may only narrow the organisation level process and must state its rationale.
