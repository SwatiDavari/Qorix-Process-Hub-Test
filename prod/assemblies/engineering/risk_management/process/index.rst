Risk Management
###############

Identify, analyse, treat and monitor risks to projects and to the organisation level processes.

* **Assembly:** Engineering / Risk Management (code ``risk``)
* **Status:** :bdg-secondary:`draft`
* **Enabler / Scope / Layer:** - / GLOB / -
* **Owner:** :need:`rl_qx_risk_risk_manager`
* **Standards:** ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 6.3.4, ASPICE 4.0 MAN.5

.. doc_getstrt:: Getting started on Risk Management
   :id: doc_getstrt_qx_risk_getting_started
   :status: valid
   :version: 1
   :tags: risk_management

   This describes how risks are identified, rated, treated and monitored at organisation level.

   A risk that affects only one project belongs in that project's register. Use this process for risks that reach several projects or the organisation level processes.

.. outcome:: Risks are identified and rated
   :id: oc_qx_risk_risks_known
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: risk_management
   :complies: std_req_iso15288_634, std_req_aspice_40_man_5

   Risks to the organisation level processes and to projects are identified, rated by likelihood and impact, and recorded in a register with an owner.

.. outcome:: Risks are treated and monitored
   :id: oc_qx_risk_risks_treated
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: risk_management
   :complies: std_req_iso15288_634, std_req_aspice_40_man_5

   Each risk above the agreed threshold has a treatment with an owner and a date, and the register is reviewed at a defined cadence.
