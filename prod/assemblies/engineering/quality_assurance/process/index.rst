Quality Assurance
#################

Quality management system and quality assurance: make sure processes are followed and work products meet their criteria.

* **Status:** :bdg-secondary:`draft`

.. doc_concept:: Quality Assurance Process
   :id: doc_concept_qx_qa_process
   :status: draft
   :version: 1
   :tags: quality_assurance

   **Purpose:** Ensure the QMS is maintained and continuously improved through
   independent audit evidence. Every Qorix project records whether its processes
   are actually applied, not merely written down.

   **Scope:** All Qorix projects and organisation-level shared assets, from the
   point at which process definitions exist.

   **Standard alignment:** ASPICE 4.0 SUP.1 (Quality Assurance).

   **Workflow sequence:**

   1. **Conduct Organisation Level Internal Audit** — scheduled at least once per
      project per year; produces the Audit Report that feeds into step 2.
   2. **Maintain Quality Management System** — annual; consumes the Audit Report
      and produces an updated QMS Plan.

   The two workflows form a closed improvement loop: audit findings drive QMS
   updates, and the updated QMS governs the next audit cycle.
