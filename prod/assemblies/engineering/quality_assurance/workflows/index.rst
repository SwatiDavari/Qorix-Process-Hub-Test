   :output: wp_qx_qa_audit_report

   The Quality Office schedules, performs (or commissions) and tracks to closure
   at least one organisation level internal audit per project per year.

   **Activities:**

   1. Schedule the audit against the QMS plan cadence.
   2. Execute the audit against the declared QMS disciplines (quality,
      safety, security).
   3. Record findings as non-conformities or observations with owners and due dates.
   4. Track actions to closure; escalate unresolved items.

   The cybersecurity scope — judging independently whether organisational processes
   achieve ISO/SAE 21434 objectives — is evaluated as part of this audit, with the
   Cybersecurity Office supporting the assessment.

   At least once per year, the Quality Office reviews aggregated internal audit
   findings and quality metrics and updates the QMS accordingly.

   **Activities:**

   1. Aggregate audit reports and quality metrics from the review period.
   2. Identify improvement actions and update the QMS document.
   3. Update discipline plans (change, configuration, documentation management)
      where findings require it.
   4. Notify affected projects of changes to the organisational baseline.
   5. Obtain Head of G&C approval for the updated QMS.

   The Functional Safety and Cybersecurity Offices support where a finding reaches
   their discipline.
